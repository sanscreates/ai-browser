# Third-party notices for AI Browser's own changes

AI Browser is built on Chromium. Chromium's own third-party licenses are generated by `tools/licenses/licenses.py` and shipped in `chrome://credits`; this file doesn't replace them.

## ungoogled-chromium
We studied ungoogled-chromium (https://github.com/ungoogled-software/ungoogled-chromium, BSD-3-Clause) to learn *what* to change. Every change in this repo was written by us against our own Chromium tree.

We reuse **none** of their code, patch files, scripts or list files:
- `aib/domains/domains.txt` and `aib/domains/files.list` were derived from our tree with `aib/scripts/scrub_domains.py survey|generate`. `google.*` expands to Chromium's own `components/google/core/common/google_tld_list.h`.
- `aib/prune/files.list` was generated by `aib/scripts/prune_binaries.py generate`.
- The flag and feature names are our own (`aib-*`).

If a future change reuses a non-trivial piece of their code or data, its BSD-3 notice goes here:

> Copyright (c) 2015-2026, The ungoogled-chromium Authors. All rights reserved.
> (BSD-3-Clause; full text at https://github.com/ungoogled-software/ungoogled-chromium/blob/master/LICENSE)

No such reuse exists today.
